## Platform

web

## Users
- **B2B Business Clients & Logistics Managers:** Manufacturing plants, retail chains, Modern Trade suppliers, and E-Commerce enterprises needing reliable on-demand or dedicated contract freight across Thailand.
- **Independent Truck Drivers & Fleet Contractors:** Professional owner-operators and drivers (4-wheel, 6-wheel, 10-wheel, trailer, and container prime movers) seeking steady routes, reliable pay, and partnership with top logistics networks (like KEX), as well as candidates seeking full-time company driver employment.
- **Operations & Dispatch Administrators:** Internal back-office team managing driver vetting, background checks, and assigning drivers to strategic logistics partners (e.g., KEX).

## Product Purpose
Provide an all-in-one corporate and logistics web platform for **5 August Transport Company Limited (บริษัท 5 สิงหา ทรานสปอร์ต จำกัด)** that establishes brand authority, showcases full-scale fleet capabilities across 7 vehicle categories, provides an interactive freight cost estimator, streamlines driver recruitment, and equips administrators with a secure back-office dashboard to manage driver allotments to strategic partners.

## Positioning
A trusted nationwide logistics enterprise combining modern technology (real-time 24/7 GPS fleet monitoring and speed compliance) with proven reliability (99.8% on-time delivery rate) and established strategic logistics alliances (such as KEX).

## Operating Context
24/7 logistics hub operations based in Samut Prakan / Greater Bangkok, nationwide cross-provincial long-haul freight (77 provinces), seaport container haulage (Laem Chabang / Khlong Toei), and Hub-to-Hub parcel distribution networks.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Public Corporate Landing Page featuring Hero with trust metrics, 6 Core Value Propositions, 7 Fleet Categories with specifications, Freight Cost Estimator, About Us with 24/7 Control Tower narrative, and Milestones timeline.
- Driver Recruitment Workflow with client-side & server-side Zod validation, mock document upload, and celebratory feedback modal.
- Password-protected Admin Back-Office (`/admin`) with real-time operational metric cards (Total, Pending, Approved, Assigned to KEX), application data table with search and filtering, applicant dossier modal, partner assignment workflow (default KEX), and partner registry management.
- Backend built with Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Server Actions, REST API routes, and Prisma ORM with SQLite (`dev.db`).
